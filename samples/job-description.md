Senior React Developer (Frontend) – Northwind Labs

About Northwind Labs
Northwind Labs builds the customer platform used by more than 200 retail and logistics businesses across Bangladesh. Our web app lets store owners track orders, manage stock and see daily sales. We are a team of 40, with 9 engineers in our Dhaka office, and we ship to production several times a week.

The role
We are hiring a Senior React Developer to lead the frontend of our customer dashboard. You will own the frontend architecture, raise the quality bar through code review and testing, and mentor two mid-level engineers. This is a hybrid role based in Dhaka, Bangladesh: 3 days a week in our Gulshan office, 2 days from home.

What you'll do
- Build and maintain the customer dashboard in React and TypeScript
- Move our public pages and onboarding flow to Next.js (server-side rendering for speed and SEO)
- Own frontend architecture: state management, routing, data fetching and error handling
- Write and maintain automated tests with Jest and React Testing Library; keep coverage of critical flows above 80%
- Work with our designer to turn Figma files into accessible (WCAG 2.1 AA), responsive UI
- Improve performance: bundle size, Core Web Vitals, slow-network behaviour
- Review pull requests and mentor two mid-level frontend engineers
- Work with backend engineers on API contracts (we use REST today and are adding GraphQL)

Must-have requirements
- 5+ years of professional frontend development
- Strong React (hooks, context, performance optimisation)
- Strong TypeScript in production code
- Hands-on experience with Next.js
- Writing automated tests with Jest and/or React Testing Library
- Experience reviewing code or mentoring other engineers

Nice to have
- GraphQL (Apollo or similar)
- Node.js for small backend or tooling work
- Building or maintaining a design system / component library
- Accessibility (WCAG) experience
- Cypress or Playwright for end-to-end tests

What we offer
- Salary: BDT 250,000 – 350,000 per month, plus a yearly performance bonus
- Health insurance for you and your family
- Learning budget of BDT 60,000 per year
- 20 days of paid leave, plus public holidays

How to apply
Send your CV and a link to your GitHub, portfolio or LinkedIn profile. We reply to every applicant within two weeks.
